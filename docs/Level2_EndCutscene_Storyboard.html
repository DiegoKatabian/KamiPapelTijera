For Valen: what to draw

Five 1920x1080 frames, same pipeline as Level 1's ending: these rough panels are only blocking (who, where, which way they move, camera distance). Reuse Level 1's photo-frame border and paint style.
  • Red arrows = direction of movement. Dashed line = path of travel. Star burst = impact.
  • Kami and the Abuela are drawn as dummies here (Kami: two buns + bow, scissors; Abuela: bun + round glasses + robe). Use the real character sheets.
  • Continuity: the lamp is the big BLUE lamp of the Narrator's desk (it already peeks in at the corner of Level 1's frame 4). The two books on the desk are Book 1 and Book 2. Book 3's title is the last thing on screen.
  • The book title is in Spanish exactly as designed: KAMI: FOTOS Y RECUERDOS. Translations of the cover, if any, are a later call.

The sequence at a glance

Beats: fly (1) → hit the lamp (2, 3) → tumble (4) → land on Book 3 (5, hold).

Industry format, in one paragraph

A storyboard is a grid of frames in the film's aspect ratio, in story order. Each frame carries a number, a shot size (WS wide, MS medium, CU close-up), the camera angle/move, the action in one sentence, the dialogue/caption, the sound, the duration and the transition to the next frame. Movement is drawn with arrows, camera moves with a second frame outline. Below, every panel follows exactly that.
Shot 01 · LiftoffWS · eye level · static
SizeWide shot (WS)
CameraLocked off, eye level. Girls cross frame left → right, small in the upper third.
ActionKami and the Abuela sail over the newspaper's rooftops toward the gutter of their book, at the right of frame.
SoundCatapult whoosh tail, wind, paper flutter.
DurationHeld until the player advances (click-to-advance, like Level 1).
TransitionCut.
Draw notesRooftops are newsprint (columns of text, headlines). The book's fold is huge and far away: it sells the scale.

SizeClose-up (CU)

SizeMedium shot (MS)

Shot 05 · Book 3WS · high angle · slow push-in · HOLD

SoundSoft thump, dust puff, then quiet. Music resolves.
